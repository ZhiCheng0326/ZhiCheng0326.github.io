Hello, I’m Zhi Cheng, an Applied AI Engineer at Z.AI (Zhipu AI). My recent work focuses on Agent Harness development and enterprise agent applications, spanning multi-agent orchestration, tool integration, and execution feedback loops. I lead projects from technical design and implementation to customer POC validation, working with domain experts to translate business requirements into executable workflows.

My earlier research explored reasoning-enhanced retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and neuro-symbolic reasoning for knowledge-based question answering (KBQA). I also have hands-on experience in constructing training data, applying supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and evaluating LLMs for complex question answering.

I earned my Master’s Degree in [Computer Science and Technology](https://www.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/) from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) in 2025, where I was a member of the [KEG Lab](https://keg.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/team), supervised by [Prof. Juanzi Li](https://keg.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/persons/ljz/). I received my bachelor’s degree in Automation from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/).

Before my master’s studies, I worked as an AI Engineer at Wise AI Malaysia on face anti-spoofing and voice cloning. My earlier research explored semi-supervised learning and graph neural networks.
